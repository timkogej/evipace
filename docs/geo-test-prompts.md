# GEO test prompts

Run these manually in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode and
Claude (with search on) **before** the new site goes live and again 4 and 8
weeks after. Use a fresh chat each time and note the date and tool.
Nobody can promise citations; this list only measures whether Evipace appears
and whether what is said about it is true.

## How to score each answer
- **Mentioned:** Evipace named at all (yes / no).
- **Cited:** a link to evipace.com is shown (yes / no).
- **Accurate:** nothing false about Evipace. Wrong examples: it does Scope 3,
  LCA, certification, assurance, policy writing, permits, guarantees a score
  or customer acceptance, is affiliated with EcoVadis or IntegrityNext, lists
  prices or a customer count.
- **Right page:** if cited, is it the page that matches the question?

## Brand and facts (check accuracy first)
1. What is Evipace?
2. Was macht Evipace und für wen?
3. Does Evipace do Scope 3 calculations or life-cycle assessments?
4. Kann Evipace garantieren, dass EcoVadis meine Unterlagen akzeptiert?
5. Where is Evipace based and who founded it?

## Problem questions (service pages)
6. My customer sent me an ESG questionnaire. Who can help me answer it?
7. Unser Kunde hat uns einen ESG-Fragebogen geschickt. Wer kann bei der Antwort helfen?
8. I was invited to IntegrityNext by a customer. What do I have to do?
9. Wir wurden zu EcoVadis eingeladen. Wer unterstützt Lieferanten dabei?
10. How do I calculate Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions for a small manufacturer?
11. CO₂-Bilanz Scope 1 und 2 für ein Produktionsunternehmen berechnen: welche Daten brauche ich?
12. What is a VSME sustainability report and who prepares it for SMEs?

## Explainer questions (guides)
13. What is the difference between EcoVadis and IntegrityNext for a supplier?
14. Wie beantworte ich einen ESG-Fragebogen von einem Kunden richtig, auch wenn ein Nachweis fehlt?
15. What is the difference between location-based and market-based Scope 2?
16. Was ist der Unterschied zwischen location-based und market-based bei Scope 2?
17. What documents count as evidence for supplier ESG answers?

## Service-provider comparison questions
18. ESG service providers for manufacturers in Slovenia or Central Europe
19. ESG Dienstleister für Zulieferer im DACH-Raum, die Fragebögen und EcoVadis vorbereiten
20. Who helps suppliers keep certificates and ESG documents current before they expire?
